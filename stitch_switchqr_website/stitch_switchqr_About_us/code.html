Why We Built This

We were tired of seeing beautiful print campaigns ruined by a single typo in a URL or a website migration that broke thousands of codes.

QR codes are a bridge between the physical and digital worlds, but for too long, that bridge was made of fragile glass. We wanted to build it out of carbon fiber.

Enter SwitchQR: The "Smarter Behavior" engine.

Same QR. Smarter behavior.

Our platform allows you to keep the same physical code while evolving the digital destination behind it indefinitely. Update links in real-time without touching the printer.
