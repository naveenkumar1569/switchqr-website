The Challenge

RetailX, a leading lifestyle brand with over 50 brick-and-mortar locations, was facing a recurring issue with their in-store marketing collateral. Every quarter, thousands of shelf-talkers and posters were printed with QR codes linking to specific seasonal landing pages.

The problem arose when marketing strategies shifted mid-quarter. Whether it was a stockout of a promoted item or a sudden pivot to a new campaign, the printed QR codes became obsolete instantly.

  • close High reprinting costs for every campaign pivot.
  • close Customer frustration leading to dead 404 links.
  • close Zero visibility into which store locations were driving traffic.
“

SwitchQR didn't just save us printing costs; it gave us a new channel for customer data we didn't know we were missing. We can now optimize our in-store traffic in real-time.

Sarah Jenkins Chief Marketing Officer, RetailX

The Solution

RetailX implemented SwitchQR to replace all static QR codes with dynamic ones. This allowed their central marketing team to update the destination URL of any code in any store, instantly, without reprinting a single piece of paper.

They utilized SwitchQR's folder management to organize codes by region, allowing regional managers to run localized promotions while HQ maintained brand consistency.

edit Dynamic Linking Changed destination URLs 4x per season based on inventory.
experiment A/B Testing Tested product video vs. reviews on landing pages.

The Result

The results were immediate. By ensuring that customers always landed on a relevant, in-stock product page, the bounce rate dropped significantly. The ability to A/B test landing pages meant that RetailX could refine the user journey week-over-week.

Beyond the 40% increase in scan rates, the operational savings were massive. The print budget for shelf-talkers was reduced by 30% annually, as generic "Scan for Deal" signage could now be reused for multiple different campaigns throughout the year.
